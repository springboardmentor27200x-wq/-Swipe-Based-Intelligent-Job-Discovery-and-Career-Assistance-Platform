# SwipeX – AI Swipe-Based Intelligent Job Discovery and Career Assistance Platform

## Overview

SwipeX is an intelligent job discovery and career assistance platform designed to make the job-search process faster, simpler, and more personalized.

The platform provides a swipe-based job discovery experience where job seekers can explore job opportunities based on their interests, skills, and preferences.

SwipeX also provides features for recruiters to post and manage job opportunities, while job seekers can discover jobs, apply for suitable positions, manage their profiles, and receive career-related assistance.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of SwipeX are:

- Simplify the job discovery process.
- Provide an intuitive swipe-based job browsing experience.
- Connect job seekers with relevant job opportunities.
- Allow recruiters to create and manage job postings.
- Help job seekers manage their profiles and applications.
- Provide resume analysis and ATS-related feedback.
- Improve the overall job-search experience through intelligent recommendations.
